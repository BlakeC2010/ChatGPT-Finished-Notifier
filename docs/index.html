Multi-monitor aware

If the AI chat tab is still visible, it stays quiet.

The extension checks whether the chat page is actually hidden. Keeping it visible on another monitor will not trigger a notification just because you clicked somewhere else.

Privacy by design

Your conversations are not collected.

The extension watches interface state on supported AI chat sites so it can tell when a response begins and ends. It does not store or transmit your prompts or responses.
